ITIN Business Loans in Florida: Lenders

By Editorial Staff · Research & Fact-Checking · Published October 14, 2026

Quick Answer

Florida ITIN holders can get business loans from CDFIs like Accion Opportunity Fund, microlenders like Kiva, and community credit unions, all without an SSN. You need a valid ITIN, an EIN, a business bank account, and 3-12 months of bank statements. Loan amounts range from $1,000 to $250,000 at rates starting around 8% APR.

Florida is one of the most entrepreneur-dense states in the country, and a substantial share of those businesses are run by immigrants who hold an ITIN rather than a Social Security Number. Florida is home to 3.5 million small businesses, and Hispanic business owners represent 28.7% of the workforce and 36.9% of ownership statewide. Despite that scale, access to financing remains a real problem for minority entrepreneurs: according to the Federal Reserve, only 25% of Hispanic business owners successfully secure funding through traditional banks. This guide covers every realistic loan path for Florida business owners who have an ITIN but no SSN.

Can I actually get a business loan in Florida without an SSN?

A question we hear often: yes, and Florida is a better state than most for finding ITIN-friendly lenders. The reason is simple supply and demand. Across Florida metro areas including Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and Jacksonville, there are more than 91,000 Latino-owned employer businesses, which creates enough demand that CDFIs, credit unions, and mission-driven lenders have built dedicated programs here.

No federal law prohibits lenders from extending business credit to ITIN holders. What changes is which lenders will work with you. Most large national banks still require an SSN for their standard small-business loan products. The most accessible paths are Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs), peer-to-peer platforms, and a growing number of revenue-based online lenders that underwrite on cash flow rather than credit bureau data alone. You will need a valid ITIN for the personal guarantee, plus an Employer Identification Number (EIN) for the business itself. Many CDFIs and nonprofit lenders accept an ITIN as the primary personal identifier in place of an SSN, but you will still need an EIN, a business bank account, and standard business documents.

What documents do Florida ITIN business lenders actually ask for?

Readers frequently ask: the document list is longer than a personal loan but shorter than an SBA 7(a). Here is what to assemble before you apply anywhere:

  • ITIN: your IRS assignment letter (CP565) confirming the number is active
  • EIN: your business Employer Identification Number, obtained free from the IRS
  • Government-issued photo ID: passport, matricula consular, or similar
  • Business bank statements: most lenders want 3-6 months; CDFI lenders may ask for 12
  • Business tax returns: 1-2 years if your business has been operating that long
  • Proof of business registration: Articles of Organization, DBA filing, or LLC certificate in Florida
  • Profit and loss statement: a simple spreadsheet works for microlenders
  • Proof of Florida address: a utility bill, lease, or bank statement showing your Florida address

CDFIs also typically request a business plan, especially for startups, so having a one-page summary of what your business does, who your customers are, and how you plan to repay the loan goes a long way with mission-driven lenders.

How do I qualify if my business is new or I have bad credit?

CDFI credit requirements are significantly more flexible than traditional banks. Working Solutions CDFI and Kiva have no minimum credit score requirement. If your business is under one year old, Kiva is the clearest entry point because it relies on your community network to fund your campaign rather than a traditional underwriting model.

For businesses that have been operating at least 6-12 months with consistent monthly deposits, revenue-based lenders are worth exploring. Revenue-based financing and certain working capital programs accept FICO 500+ and underwrite primarily on bank statement health. The tradeoff is cost: factor-rate pricing on these products converts to an effective APR that can run 30%-60%, so they work best for short-term working capital, not multi-year investments.

Accion Opportunity Fund requires approximately 620, but many CDFIs evaluate your full financial picture, including cash flow, business plan, and community impact, rather than relying solely on credit scores. If your score is below 620, showing 12 months of clean bank statements with no overdrafts is the single most effective way to strengthen your application.

What loan amounts and rates should I expect in Florida?

Loan size and cost vary enormously by program type. At the micro end, Kiva offers up to $15,000 at 0% interest, making it the cheapest capital available to any ITIN holder in Florida. The catch is that funding depends on your social network pledging support during the campaign period, which typically runs 15-30 days.

CDFI loans through programs like Accion Opportunity Fund go much higher. Accion offers $5,000 to $250,000, accepts ITINs, serves clients in Spanish, and more than 90% of their borrowers are women, people of color, or from low-to-moderate-income backgrounds.

For businesses that need capital faster, working capital and revenue-based financing can fund within 4-24 hours of signing for approved Florida businesses. Speed comes at a price, so use this option only when cash is needed to fill a gap in receivables or to take advantage of a time-sensitive opportunity, not as long-term financing.

What is the step-by-step process to apply for an ITIN business loan in Florida?

A question we hear often: the path is more straightforward than most people expect once you know which lender type fits your situation.

  1. Confirm your ITIN is active. Call the IRS ITIN unit or log into your IRS online account. An expired ITIN needs to be renewed before any lender will process your personal guarantee.
  2. Get your EIN if you don’t have one yet. Apply free at IRS.gov; you receive it immediately online. This is the tax ID for your business, separate from your personal ITIN.
  3. Register your Florida business. An LLC or DBA filed with the Florida Division of Corporations (sunbiz.org) gives lenders the legal entity they need to underwrite against.
  4. Open a dedicated business bank account. A business bank account with 12 months of statements and a clear business plan are among the three documents that matter most to CDFI lenders.
  5. Match yourself to the right lender tier. Startup with no revenue? Start with Kiva. Six or more months of deposits and a 600+ score? Try Accion or DreamSpring. Need funds in 48 hours? Look at revenue-based lenders.
  6. Apply and respond quickly. CDFI underwriters often ask follow-up questions; responding within 24 hours keeps your file moving. Core documents most lenders require include personal and business tax returns, recent profit and loss statements, a current balance sheet, and business bank statements for the past three to six months.

Why does Florida specifically have more ITIN-friendly business lenders?

The state’s demographics drive lender supply. About 22%-23% of Florida’s residents, roughly 5 million people, are foreign-born, which is much higher than the nationwide average of 14%. In 2025, one study found that 267,700 of those Florida immigrants, about 5%, were entrepreneurs. Where there is demand, mission-driven lenders follow. According to a UCLA analysis of federal labor statistics, about 670 out of every 100,000 immigrants launched a new business each month in 2023, compared to an overall average of 0.35% for the adult population. Florida’s CDFIs and immigrant-serving credit unions have grown their ITIN business loan programs in direct response to that entrepreneurial activity, making the state one of the most practical places in the country to access this kind of financing.

FAQs

Can I get a business loan in Florida with an ITIN and no SSN? Yes. CDFIs, microlenders like Kiva, and some credit unions accept an ITIN as the primary personal identifier. You will still need an EIN for your business, a business bank account, and proof of revenue or income.

What is the minimum credit score to get a Florida ITIN business loan? It depends on the lender. Kiva has no minimum credit score. DreamSpring requires 600+. Accion Opportunity Fund suggests around 620. Revenue-based online lenders often accept 500+ if your bank deposits are strong.

How much can I borrow with an ITIN business loan in Florida? Kiva offers up to $15,000 at 0% interest. SBA microloan intermediaries lend up to $50,000. Accion Opportunity Fund goes up to $250,000. Revenue-based working capital lenders may fund $25,000 to $500,000 based on monthly deposits.

Do I need to be a U.S. citizen to get a business loan in Florida? No. Florida lenders, particularly CDFIs and microlenders, do not require citizenship or a green card. A valid ITIN, an EIN, and documented business activity are the core requirements.

How long does it take to get approved for a Florida ITIN business loan? Kiva campaigns typically fund over 15-30 days. CDFI loans through Accion or DreamSpring usually take 1-4 weeks from application to funding. Revenue-based online lenders can fund in 24-72 hours once documents are verified.
