ITIN Mortgage in Florida: Rates

By Research Desk · Lenders & Loan Programs · Published August 28, 2026

Advertiser disclosure:ITIN Lending is an independent resource. Some links on this page are from partners who compensate us, which may affect which products we feature and where. This does not change our editorial guidance and is never a cost to you. See our full disclosure.

Quick Answer

ITIN holders can get a mortgage in Florida without an SSN. Most Florida ITIN mortgage programs require 10%-20% down, a minimum credit score of 620 (or alternative credit references), two years of tax returns filed under your ITIN, and stable income history. Rates run roughly 0.5%-2% above conventional loans.

Florida has one of the largest immigrant communities in the country, and thousands of ITIN holders across Miami-Dade, Orange, Hillsborough, and Broward counties want to own the homes they already live in. The good news: you do not need a Social Security number to get a mortgage here.

If you live and work in the U.S., file taxes under an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, and have no Social Security number, you can still buy a Florida home. This guide explains exactly how.

Does Florida actually have ITIN mortgage programs, or is this just marketing?

ITIN mortgage programs are real, funded loans, not bait-and-switch advertising. They remain available in 2026 and have expanded in Florida, with more competitive rates and more flexible products than in prior years.

What you will not find are federally backed FHA or Fannie Mae loans: those require an SSN. ITIN mortgages come primarily from non-QM specialty lenders, credit unions serving immigrant communities, portfolio banks, and mortgage brokers with access to wholesale lenders. Large national banks generally do not offer them.

The practical implication: you need to seek out lenders who specialize in this product. A mortgage broker who knows non-QM lending can shop your file across multiple ITIN-friendly lenders in a single application, which matters because pricing varies sharply from one lender to the next.

What are the basic requirements to qualify for an ITIN mortgage in Florida?

A question we hear often: what does the lender actually look at if you have no SSN?

Every lender is slightly different, but here is what most ITIN mortgage programs in Florida require in 2026.

  • Valid ITIN. Your ITIN must be active and on file with your most recent tax returns.
  • Two years of tax returns. Two years of U.S. tax returns filed under your ITIN, plus pay stubs or self-employment documentation.
  • Employment history. Lenders typically expect a steady employment history for at least two years.
  • Down payment. Most ITIN programs require a 10% minimum; 20% gets you better rates and removes PMI.
  • Credit history. U.S. credit or alternative credit (rent receipts, utility bills, foreign credit reports) are accepted.
  • Proof of income. Bank statements (12-24 months), W-2s, or profit and loss statements for self-employed buyers all qualify.
  • Government-issued photo ID. A passport or consular ID (Matricula Consular accepted by some lenders) satisfies this requirement.

If you are self-employed, many ITIN borrowers use a bank statement program rather than tax returns as their primary income document. Lenders review 12-24 months of bank deposits to calculate qualifying income. Personal bank statements usually apply a standard expense factor of around 50%, meaning half of the average monthly deposits count as usable income.

What credit score do I need, and what if I have no U.S. credit history at all?

This one comes up a lot: many Florida ITIN borrowers have never opened a U.S. credit card or taken out a U.S. loan, so their FICO file is thin or nonexistent.

Most ITIN mortgage loans in Florida require a minimum credit score between 620 and 660. Some lenders will go as low as 580, but typically only when the borrower brings a larger down payment or additional cash reserves to compensate.

No U.S. score at all does not automatically disqualify you. Many ITIN lenders accept alternative credit references, rental payments, utility bills, and insurance payments in place of traditional scores. They generally want at least 3-4 references with 12 months of on-time payment history.

The fastest way to build a qualifying credit file before applying is to open a secured credit card using your ITIN. See our guide to building credit with an ITIN for a step-by-step plan.

How do ITIN mortgage rates in Florida compare to conventional rates?

ITIN mortgage rates generally run 0.5%-2% above conventional loan rates, and down payment requirements are higher. In 2026, Florida ITIN mortgage rates range from the mid-7s to the low-9s depending on your credit profile, down payment, loan amount, and property type.

Several factors move your rate in either direction:

FactorRate Impact
Credit score 700+Better pricing, sometimes 0.5% lower
Credit score below 640Higher rate, possible extra lender fees
Down payment 20%+Improved pricing, no PMI
Down payment 10%-15%Standard non-QM rate tier
12+ months cash reservesCompensates for thinner credit file
Bank statement income (vs. tax returns)May add a small rate premium
Loan amount above $1M (jumbo)Higher rate, stricter reserve requirement

Best pricing kicks in with a 700+ FICO score, 25% or more down, and 12 or more months of reserves. The CFPB notes that some lenders have mortgage programs for ITIN holders, though borrowers may need to pay a higher down payment and a higher interest rate than on traditional loans. Shopping at least three lenders before committing is not optional when rate spreads can be this wide.

What kinds of Florida properties can I buy with an ITIN mortgage?

Readers frequently ask: does it matter if I want a condo in Miami versus a single-family home in Tampa?

ITIN loans work for primary residences, second homes, investment properties, and 1-4 unit owner-occupied buildings. They cover single-family residences, townhomes, PUDs, and both warrantable and non-warrantable condos. Some lenders also finance manufactured homes and 2-4 unit multifamily properties. Maximum loan amounts vary by lender; some programs go up to $2,500,000.

One advantage ITIN loans carry over foreign national loans: unlike foreign national programs, which are often limited to investment or vacation properties, ITIN loans let you buy a primary residence. Your ITIN status actually opens more doors here.

For ITIN holders who already own in Florida and want to tap equity, see our guides on ITIN home equity loans and HELOCs with an ITIN.

What down payment do I realistically need in Florida in 2026?

The practical breakdown:

  • 10% down: Available from select lenders for borrowers with strong credit (640+) and solid reserves. Least common and comes with the highest rate.
  • 15%-20% down: The most common range for Florida ITIN mortgage approvals in 2026. Most programs land somewhere in this window depending on the borrower’s profile.
  • 25%+ down: Opens the most competitive rates and the broadest lender choices.

For a $350,000 home at 20% down, that means $70,000 in cash at closing, plus closing costs of roughly 2%-4% of the loan amount. Down payment assistance programs do exist for ITIN borrowers in Florida. See our down payment assistance guide for programs that may reduce that upfront number.

How long does the ITIN mortgage process take in Florida?

With proper documentation, the process typically takes 30-45 days from pre-approval to closing. Pre-approval itself can usually be issued within 3-5 business days once all documents are received.

The single biggest delay is missing or incomplete documentation. Having your ITIN tax returns, bank statements, and ID ready before you call significantly speeds things up. Lenders who specialize in ITIN loans also process files more efficiently than generalist lenders encountering this loan type for the first time. Non-QM underwriting requires familiarity with alternative documentation, and working with lenders who handle it regularly means fewer surprises and clearer timelines.

What are the most common reasons ITIN mortgage applications get denied in Florida?

A question we hear often: what kills an application after the borrower seemed qualified?

Most denials come down to a short list of predictable problems. Knowing them early makes it easier to fix things before underwriting begins.

  • Inconsistent income: Large gaps in employment or irregular bank deposits that cannot be clearly explained or documented.
  • Missing ITIN tax filings: Failure to file U.S. tax returns using the ITIN in recent years, or gaps in filing history.
  • No verifiable credit history: No traditional credit score and insufficient alternative credit documentation to compensate.
  • Insufficient down payment: Not enough funds to cover the minimum down payment, closing costs, and required reserves.

If your ITIN has expired and you have missed recent filing years, renew it first. Our ITIN renewal guide walks through the process with the IRS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy a house in Florida with an ITIN and no Social Security number? Yes. A growing number of Florida lenders offer non-QM ITIN mortgage programs that accept your ITIN in place of an SSN. You will still need to document income, supply two years of tax returns filed under your ITIN, and provide a down payment of at least 10%-20%.

What credit score do I need for an ITIN mortgage in Florida? Most Florida ITIN mortgage programs require a minimum score of 620-660. Some lenders accept scores as low as 580-600 with compensating factors like a larger down payment or strong reserves. If you have no U.S. credit score, many lenders will accept alternative credit references such as 12+ months of on-time rent, utility, or phone-bill payments.

How much down payment is required for an ITIN mortgage in Florida? Most programs require 15%-20% down. Some specialized lenders accept 10% minimum, especially for borrowers with strong credit and reserves. Putting 20% or more down typically unlocks better rates and eliminates private mortgage insurance.

What documents do I need to apply for an ITIN mortgage in Florida? You generally need your IRS ITIN assignment letter (CP565 or similar), two years of federal tax returns filed under your ITIN, recent pay stubs or 12-24 months of bank statements if self-employed, a government-issued photo ID (passport or Matricula Consular), proof of residency, and 12+ months of alternative credit references if you lack a U.S. credit score.

How long does it take to close an ITIN mortgage in Florida? With complete documentation ready before you apply, most Florida ITIN lenders can issue a pre-approval within 3-5 business days. Full closing typically takes 30-45 days from pre-approval, comparable to a conventional mortgage timeline.

What types of Florida properties can I finance with an ITIN mortgage? ITIN mortgage programs in Florida generally cover single-family homes, townhomes, condominiums, and 1-4 unit owner-occupied properties. Some lenders also finance second homes and investment properties. Manufactured homes on land are accepted by select lenders.
