ITIN Personal Loan Bad Credit: TX, CA & FL Guide (2026)

Quick Answer

Yes. ITIN holders with bad or no credit can get personal loans in Texas, California, and Florida through lenders like Oportun, certain credit unions, and ITIN-friendly online lenders. Expect APRs of 29%-36%, loan amounts of $300-$10,000, and approval based on income rather than credit score alone.

Getting a personal loan with an ITIN and less-than-perfect credit is possible, but the path is not the same in every state. Texas, California, and Florida have the largest concentrations of ITIN holders in the country, and each state has its own mix of community lenders, credit unions, and online options worth knowing. This guide covers who approves these loans in 2026, what the real costs look like, and how to strengthen your application before you apply.

Can someone with an ITIN really get a personal loan with bad credit?

A question we hear often:

Yes. Your ITIN is not the obstacle most people assume it is.

Personal loans with an ITIN do exist, but not every lender offers them, not every borrower profile qualifies, and not every offer is worth accepting. The bigger friction points are usually limited U.S. credit history, income that is hard to document, or an application that does not match what the lender needs to see.

A growing number of lenders now evaluate ITIN borrowers on income and payment patterns rather than a credit score alone. Some will approve borrowers with no traditional credit score at all, using alternative history instead: rent payments, utility bills, other documented records. If you have been paying your rent, phone bill, or utilities on time, that track record carries real weight with the right lender.

One thing worth keeping in mind: a thin credit file is not the same as bad credit. Many ITIN holders simply have no U.S. credit score yet. Lenders who work in this space understand that distinction, and it opens more doors than many applicants expect.

Which lenders approve ITIN personal loans for bad-credit borrowers in 2026?

The clearest option for bad-credit ITIN borrowers is Oportun. To apply, you need a valid photo ID and a Social Security number or ITIN. Oportun sets no minimum credit score and will lend to a thin or no-credit file. Borrowers can access between $300 and $10,000, repaid in fixed monthly installments with rates capped at 35.99% APR.

Two other categories are worth exploring:

Credit unions with ITIN programs. Security Service Federal Credit Union, for example, offers personal loans to ITIN holders ranging from $250 to $50,000 with an APR range of 9.99%-17.95%. Credit unions typically require membership, but many have open eligibility requirements. Anyone who lives in the United States or a U.S. territory can become eligible to join Security Service FCU through a free membership with the American Consumer Council.

Larger banks with existing relationships. Wells Fargo accepts ITINs for personal loan applications, offers one of the lower APR ranges, and can lend larger amounts, but you need a Wells Fargo account open for at least 12 months to qualify.

Is it harder to get approved in Texas, California, or Florida specifically?

This one comes up a lot:

State law shapes what lenders can charge and how they operate, which affects availability. All three states have solid ITIN lending coverage.

Texas has a large and well-developed market for ITIN borrowers. ITIN loans are most common in areas with high immigrant or Latino populations, including California, Florida, Texas, Oklahoma, and Illinois. Many community development financial institutions (CDFIs) and credit unions in Texas serve ITIN holders specifically, and Oportun operates there. Secured personal loans from some lenders are available in California, Florida, Texas, New Jersey, Arizona, Illinois, Nevada, and Utah, so Texas borrowers have access to both secured and unsecured options.

California has some of the most ITIN-friendly lenders in the country, including Oportun (founded in California) and numerous credit unions serving Latino communities. The California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) licenses and oversees consumer lenders, which means you can verify any lender’s registration before you sign anything.

Florida also has strong coverage. Oportun offers personal loans in 40 states but is not available in Colorado, Connecticut, Iowa, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Nevada, New York, West Virginia, or Washington, D.C. Florida is included in Oportun’s coverage area, as are Texas and California.

What documents do I need to apply for an ITIN personal loan with bad credit?

Readers frequently ask:

The exact list varies by lender, but most ITIN personal loan applications in 2026 require:

  • Your ITIN letter from the IRS (or the physical card if you have one)
  • A valid government-issued photo ID (passport, consular ID, or state ID)
  • Proof of income: recent pay stubs, bank statements showing deposits, or tax returns
  • Proof of address: a utility bill, lease agreement, or bank statement with your current address
  • In some cases, two personal or employment references

Most ITIN-friendly lenders let you apply online or by phone with an ID and proof of income. Gathering these documents before you start speeds up approval considerably. If you are self-employed, plan to provide 12 months of bank statements rather than pay stubs, since lenders need to see consistent deposit patterns.

One practical step many borrowers skip: confirm that your ITIN has not expired. Most lenders will not process a loan application until the ITIN is current, because they need it to pull any existing credit file. If yours needs updating, see our guide on how to renew your ITIN number before applying.

How do APRs and loan amounts compare for bad-credit ITIN borrowers?

Expect rates to run higher than what you see advertised for prime borrowers. Some lenders charge triple-digit rates on bad-credit borrowers. Oportun charges a flat 35.95% for unsecured loans, which sits just under the threshold many financial experts consider a warning sign for predatory lending. In this segment, that is a meaningful ceiling.

Oportun’s loans run from $300 to $10,000, with APRs of 29%-35.95% and origination fees of 0%-8%. If you can qualify at a credit union, rates drop significantly. Security Service Federal Credit Union offers loan amounts from $250 to $50,000 with an APR range of 9.99%-17.95%, though credit union membership and a modest credit history may be required.

Adding a co-signer is one of the most effective ways to lower your rate. Oportun allows co-signers, and a stronger co-signer profile can improve both your approval odds and your rate. Just make sure the co-signer understands that late payments affect their credit as much as yours.

Will this loan help me build credit for the future?

Yes, as long as you choose a lender that reports to the major bureaus. Oportun reports to all three, so on-time payments build the file you may not have yet. If the lender reports your payment behavior, paying on time can move your score in the right direction over time.

That is one of the stronger long-term reasons to take an ITIN personal loan even at a higher rate, especially if you plan to apply for a mortgage or auto loan later. Building 12-24 months of positive payment history can move a borrower from no score to somewhere in the 600s, which means meaningfully better terms on future products. For a structured approach to that process, see our guide on building credit with an ITIN.

According to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), payment history is the single most heavily weighted factor in standard credit scoring models, accounting for roughly 35% of a FICO score. A personal loan repaid on time is one of the fastest ways to establish a U.S. credit profile from scratch.

What are the biggest mistakes to avoid when applying?

A question we hear often:

Two mistakes come up constantly. The first is applying at multiple lenders in a short window without pre-qualifying first. The second is choosing the first lender that says yes without comparing total loan cost.

Oportun lets you check your approval odds and potential rates online before you formally apply, and the pre-qualification process does not affect your credit score. Most other ITIN-friendly lenders offer the same soft-pull step. Use it at two or three lenders before committing to a hard inquiry.

The second mistake is fixating on monthly payment rather than APR. A lower monthly payment can mean a longer term and far more interest paid overall. On a $5,000 loan at 35.95% APR over 36 months, you would pay roughly $3,000 in interest. The same loan at 17% APR over 36 months costs about $1,500 in interest. That $1,500 difference is worth the extra effort of joining a credit union or improving your application profile before you apply.

If your credit is too damaged right now to qualify for a standard personal loan, a credit builder loan with an ITIN is the recommended first step. It requires no existing credit score, deposits the funds into a locked account, and builds your file as you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a personal loan with an ITIN and bad credit in Texas? Yes. Lenders like Oportun operate in Texas and accept ITINs with no minimum credit score. Community development credit unions in Texas also offer ITIN personal loans using alternative criteria like employment history and bank statements.

What credit score do I need for an ITIN personal loan in California or Florida? Some lenders, including Oportun, set no minimum credit score for ITIN borrowers. Others may look for a score around 580-620. Alternative data such as rent payment history or utility bills can substitute for a thin file.

How much can I borrow with an ITIN personal loan if I have bad credit? Most ITIN-friendly lenders cap bad-credit personal loans at $300-$10,000. If you have a longer income history or add a co-signer, some lenders may approve up to $35,000.

Will applying for an ITIN personal loan hurt my credit score? Pre-qualifying with most ITIN lenders uses only a soft credit pull, so it does not affect your score. A hard inquiry only occurs when you formally submit a full application.

Can a co-signer help me get approved for an ITIN personal loan with bad credit? Yes. Adding a co-signer with stronger credit can increase your approval odds and may lower your interest rate. Both you and the co-signer are equally responsible for repayment, so missed payments affect both credit files.

Do ITIN personal loans in Texas, California, or Florida build credit? They can. Lenders like Oportun report to the three major credit bureaus, so consistent on-time payments gradually build your U.S. credit history, which improves your chances of better loan terms in the future.
