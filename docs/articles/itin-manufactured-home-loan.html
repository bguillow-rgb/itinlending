ITIN Manufactured Home Loan: How to Finance in 2026

Quick Answer

Yes. ITIN holders can finance a manufactured home through Non-QM portfolio lenders that accept an ITIN in place of an SSN. Most programs require 10%-20% down, two years of tax returns filed with your ITIN, and a credit score of 620 or higher, though some lenders use alternative credit for borrowers with no bureau history.

Manufactured and mobile homes are one of the most affordable paths to homeownership in the United States, and ITIN holders buy them every day. The challenge is finding lenders willing to process the file without a Social Security Number. This guide walks through exactly how those loans work in 2026, what they cost, and how to put yourself in the strongest position before you apply.

Can I really buy a manufactured home with just an ITIN, no SSN?

Yes, and it is more common than many borrowers realize.

An ITIN mortgage loan is a home loan for buyers who use an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number issued by the IRS instead of a Social Security Number. Most traditional loans, including those backed by Fannie Mae or FHA, have strict SSN and residency requirements, so ITIN loans fall under a category called Non-QM (non-qualified mortgage) loans.

For manufactured housing specifically, the pool of willing lenders is smaller than for site-built homes, but it is real. Lenders such as First National Bank of America (FNBA) and Manufactured Nationwide (through The Federal Savings Bank) openly advertise ITIN manufactured home programs. FNBA finances single-family homes, condominiums, townhomes, some mobile/manufactured homes, and 2-4 unit multifamily properties. Manufactured Nationwide lists ITIN home loans as a specialty program alongside FHA, VA, and USDA options.

The key condition nearly every lender imposes: the manufactured home must be treated as real property, not personal property. That means it must be permanently affixed to a foundation on land you own or are purchasing alongside the home.

What is the difference between a manufactured home mortgage and a chattel loan?

A question we hear often: borrowers sometimes arrive having been quoted a “chattel loan” and wonder if that is their only option as an ITIN holder.

A chattel loan finances personal property such as vehicles and mobile homes. Compared to a traditional mortgage, chattel loans tend to have easier approval requirements but come with tradeoffs, including the risk of asset seizure upon default. Higher rates and shorter loan terms also mean higher monthly payments, despite the simpler application process.

A manufactured home mortgage treats the home as real estate. Most lenders require the home to have been built after June 15, 1976 (HUD code compliance), permanently affixed to a foundation, and classified as real property rather than personal property. The borrower must own or be purchasing the land beneath the home.

For ITIN holders, the mortgage route is almost always preferable. Rates are lower, terms run up to 30 years, and the loan builds real equity. If a lender only offers you a chattel loan, shop further before signing.

What do lenders look for when underwriting an ITIN manufactured home loan?

This one comes up a lot: understanding underwriting criteria ahead of time lets you prepare the right documents and avoid surprises.

Most Non-QM portfolio lenders evaluate five main factors:

  1. Valid, current ITIN. The ITIN must be active. ITINs that have not been used on a federal tax return for three consecutive years expire and must be renewed through the IRS using Form W-7 before a loan can move forward.

  2. Tax history. Lenders typically want two years of federal tax returns filed under your ITIN, along with an IRS ITIN letter.

  3. Income documentation. Tax returns or bank statements are the standard. Self-employed borrowers often qualify through a bank-statement program using 12-24 months of deposits.

  4. Credit profile. A bureau score of 620-660 is the most common floor. Some lenders accept alternative credit, such as rent payment records or utility history, in place of a bureau score, but they will require a larger down payment to compensate.

  5. Down payment. Most programs require 10%-20% down, with some ITIN manufactured home programs setting the floor at 15% for borrowers with thinner credit files.

How do ITIN manufactured home loan rates compare to standard mortgages?

Rates on ITIN manufactured home loans carry two separate premiums stacked on top of a conventional benchmark: one for the Non-QM structure and one for manufactured housing.

As of early 2026, manufactured home loan rates start around 6.75%. ITIN mortgage rates run 1-3 percentage points higher than conventional rates, depending on down payment, credit profile, loan amount, and lender.

In practice, a well-qualified ITIN borrower putting 20% down on a manufactured home with a 680 credit score might expect a rate in the 8.0%-9.5% range in mid-2026, depending on lender and state. Borrowers with thinner credit or lower down payments can see rates above 10%. That is a real cost, and it is worth using a rate comparison through an ITIN mortgage lender before committing to a single quote.

What property requirements must a manufactured home meet?

Readers frequently ask whether a home in a mobile home park qualifies. The short answer is: usually not for a mortgage, though chattel financing may still apply.

For an ITIN manufactured home mortgage to go through, the property itself must clear several hurdles:

  • The home must have been built on or after June 15, 1976, when federal standards for manufactured housing were established.
  • The home must be permanently affixed to a foundation and meet U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) construction and safety codes.
  • The home must be titled as real property, not as a vehicle or personal property. In most states this requires retiring the title (also called “de-titling”) and recording the home as part of the real estate parcel.
  • True home equity loans and HELOCs on manufactured homes are exception-only, for doublewide or larger units, never singlewide, and approved case by case.

If the land is leased (as in a mobile home park), most portfolio lenders will decline a mortgage application. A chattel loan may still be available, but the financing costs are higher and the terms shorter.

Are there ITIN manufactured home loan options in Texas, California, and Florida?

This one comes up a lot, especially because those three states have the highest concentration of both ITIN borrowers and manufactured housing stock.

Texas: A large number of regional credit unions and community banks in Texas actively market ITIN lending programs, and the state’s rural land-purchase culture means many manufactured homes already sit on owned land, which satisfies the real-property requirement. Our broader ITIN loans in Texas guide covers additional lender options for that state.

California: State housing finance programs through CalHFA are open to ITIN-eligible first-time buyers. For Latino ITIN holders who qualify as first-time buyers, CalHFA offers FHA loan programs consisting of 30-year fixed-rate mortgages, which can be combined with down payment and closing cost assistance. CalHFA financing can apply to manufactured homes that meet HUD code and are permanently affixed. See our ITIN loans in California guide for the full picture.

Florida: National portfolio lenders licensed in all 50 states serve Florida buyers, and the state has a high density of ITIN-friendly lenders in Miami-Dade, Orlando, and Tampa metro areas. Our ITIN loans in Florida guide lists local and statewide options.

For all three states, a national lender such as Manufactured Nationwide (The Federal Savings Bank, NMLS 411500) or Build Buy Refi offers purchase loans up to 89.99% loan-to-value, rate-and-term or cash-out refinancing, and in-house underwriting in all 50 states with no PMI and no brokering.

What if I have bad credit or no credit history at all?

A thin or nonexistent U.S. credit file is the most common obstacle ITIN borrowers face when financing a manufactured home, but it does not automatically disqualify you.

Some lenders offer ITIN mortgage loans to borrowers without a credit history, though those loans typically carry higher rates and additional requirements. The most common workaround is alternative credit documentation: a 12-24 month record of on-time rent payments, insurance premiums, utility bills, or remittances can substitute for a bureau score at select portfolio lenders. You will need signed letters from landlords or institutions, plus bank statements confirming the payments.

If your credit profile needs work before you apply, a credit builder loan with your ITIN is one of the faster ways to establish a bureau score. Consistently paying a credit builder account for 12 months can generate a scoreable file at all three major bureaus, potentially opening the door to better rates on a manufactured home loan. If you already have some credit history but it is damaged, our guide on ITIN loans with bad credit covers lender options that weigh compensating factors like income and reserves.

Step-by-step: how to apply for an ITIN manufactured home loan

  1. Confirm your ITIN is active. Check your most recent tax return. If you have not filed in the past three years, renew through Form W-7 before contacting any lender.
  2. Verify the home’s HUD compliance date. Look for the HUD certification label on the exterior. Homes built before June 15, 1976 do not qualify for mortgage financing.
  3. Confirm real-property status. Ask the county assessor or title company whether the home is titled as real estate. If it is still titled as personal property, a real estate attorney can guide the de-titling process.
  4. Gather income documents. Two years of federal tax returns, two months of bank statements, and recent pay stubs or a profit-and-loss statement for self-employed borrowers.
  5. Save for your down payment and reserves. Most lenders want to see 2-3 months of mortgage payments in reserve after your down payment clears.
  6. Get multiple quotes. Comparing several ITIN lenders and working with an experienced broker can get you a more competitive rate than going straight to a single lender.
  7. Submit a complete application. Incomplete files are the single biggest cause of delays. Have your documents organized before you apply.

Common questions

Can I get a manufactured home loan with an ITIN and no credit history?

Some Non-QM portfolio lenders accept alternative credit references such as 12-24 months of on-time rent, utility, or insurance payments in place of a bureau credit score. Expect a larger down payment, typically 20%-25%, and a higher rate to compensate for the added risk.

Do FHA loans for manufactured homes accept an ITIN?

Standard FHA loans require a valid Social Security Number and legal residency, so most ITIN-only borrowers do not qualify. A small number of lenders offer FHA-style programs with similar 3.5% down structures for ITIN holders, but these are portfolio products, not true FHA loans.

What is the difference between a chattel loan and a mortgage for a manufactured home?

A chattel loan finances the home as personal property, not real estate. It has easier approval but shorter terms (typically 15-20 years), higher rates, and no land ownership. A manufactured home mortgage treats the home as real property affixed to land you own or are purchasing, with lower rates and 30-year terms.

How much down payment do I need for an ITIN manufactured home loan?

Most ITIN programs for manufactured housing require 10%-20% down. A 10% down option is available from select lenders when the home is permanently affixed to owned land and the borrower has a credit score of 660 or higher. No-down-payment options are not typically available for ITIN manufactured home loans.

Which states have the most ITIN manufactured home loan options?

Texas, California, and Florida have the highest concentration of lenders offering ITIN manufactured home financing because those states have the largest ITIN borrower populations and the most manufactured housing stock. National portfolio lenders licensed in all 50 states are also an option regardless of your state.
