Quick Answer

Yes, you can get a car loan with an ITIN number. Credit unions like KeyPoint CU (CA), Red River CU (TX/OK), Keys Federal CU (FL), and online lenders like Lendbuzz and Beneficial State Bank all approve ITIN auto loans. Rates typically run 10.9%-24.9% APR, and most lenders require a 10%-20% down payment plus proof of income.

What exactly is an ITIN auto loan, and am I eligible?

Lenders that offer ITIN auto loans use your ITIN to verify identity and establish a credit history. You will typically need to show proof of income, residency, and tax filings. The arrangement exists specifically for people who cannot access traditional credit because of their immigration status or the absence of an SSN.

In practical terms, your ITIN replaces your Social Security number at the application stage. The lender still underwrites the loan against your income, residence history, and vehicle value. Many ITIN lenders do not require U.S. credit history at all. They look at income, employment stability, and down payment. Some will consider a credit history from your home country if you have one.

Eligibility is broad. The borrowers who typically seek ITIN financing include recent immigrants who have moved to the U.S. for work or study and do not yet have an SSN, foreign business professionals, and others with foreign status who earn U.S. income.

One concern that comes up frequently: are ITIN borrowers actually riskier? The data says no. Research from the SMU Cox School of Business found that immigrants who enter the U.S. credit system tend to have lower delinquency rates, higher credit scores, and more conservative borrowing habits than native-born consumers. Beneficial State Bank’s portfolio backs that up: ITIN loans make up about 13% of its auto loan book and have consistently posted fewer charge-offs than its standard loans.

What are the real rates on ITIN auto loans right now?

A question we hear often: “I know ITIN loans cost more, but how much more?”

ITIN auto loan rates typically range from 10.9% to 24.9% APR. The rate depends on your income, down payment, and whether you have any U.S. credit history. A larger down payment is the most direct lever you control.

To put that in context: in August 2026, Edmunds.com reported the average car loan interest rate for July 2026 as 7% APR for new vehicles and 10.6% APR for used vehicles. A well-qualified ITIN borrower at the low end of the range (around 10%-12%) is paying close to what a standard borrower pays on a used car. The spread widens considerably at the high end.

For borrowers with zero U.S. credit history, the relevant comparison is subprime lending. According to Experian’s 2025 State of the Automotive Finance Market report, the average rate for deep subprime borrowers (scores below 500) reached 21.38% for new vehicles and 22.16% for used. A good ITIN lender should beat those numbers.

The most dramatic rate improvement in the data comes from refinancing. In 2022, clients who refinanced their auto loans with Beneficial State Bank reduced their rates by an average of roughly 9%. That is why refinancing six to twelve months after establishing a U.S. payment record can be a meaningful financial move for ITIN borrowers who started with higher-rate dealer financing.

Which lenders actually approve ITIN auto loans in California?

This one comes up a lot: California has the largest concentration of ITIN holders in the U.S., which means the most active ITIN auto loan market is here.

KeyPoint Credit Union (San Jose / Bay Area): KeyPoint launched an ITIN auto loan program for borrowers who hold an ITIN instead of an SSN. Key features include competitive interest rates comparable to those offered to SSN holders, no credit history required, and flexible loan terms (including financing for purchases from a friend or family member). Membership is primarily tied to living or working in certain California communities, though joining the Financial Fitness Association at no cost is an alternative path in.

Santa Ana Federal Credit Union (Southern CA): SAFCU is an ITIN-approved lender. Once you become a member (which requires opening a savings account), you qualify for low-interest rates. You do not need to live in Santa Ana to join. SAFCU serves borrowers from all communities, including those with ITINs and immediate family members of current members. Its ITIN Lending Program covers auto loans, personal loans, and credit cards, with bilingual resources available throughout.

Beneficial State Bank: In its first year accepting ITINs for auto loans and credit cards, Beneficial State Bank made $100,000 in loans. The program grew every year after that. By 2022, the bank had $33.69 million in outstanding auto loans to 1,247 ITIN borrowers, with growth continuing into 2023. It operates in California, Oregon, and Washington.

Lendbuzz (nationwide, including CA): Lendbuzz offers auto loans without an SSN for applicants who hold an ITIN. Rather than a traditional FICO score, it uses alternative data and AI analysis to evaluate creditworthiness.

Which lenders approve ITIN auto loans in Texas?

Texas has a large and active ITIN lending market, anchored mainly by regional credit unions and dealership financing networks.

Red River Credit Union (TX / OK / AR): RRCU’s ITIN auto loans come with competitive rates, flexible terms, and credit bureau reporting, which builds your U.S. credit file with every on-time payment. Refinancing from another institution is available with a $20,000 minimum. RRCU is one of the few lenders that explicitly advertises bureau reporting for ITIN accounts, a real long-term advantage for borrowers trying to establish a U.S. credit profile. The credit union is also bilingual: “Hablamos Espanol.”

Star of Texas Credit Union (Austin area): Star of Texas offers auto loans with low rates, flexible terms, and financing up to 100% of the purchase price including taxes and fees. ITIN holders are welcome to apply. Membership eligibility applies, and a loan officer review follows the application.

ITIN-friendly dealerships across Texas: Dealer-arranged financing is another path for Texas ITIN borrowers. Networks of lenders work with dealerships in Houston, Dallas, San Antonio, and Austin that specifically market ITIN financing for new and used vehicles. These dealerships work with multiple lenders, including those who accept an ITIN in place of an SSN, even for borrowers with limited or no U.S. credit history.

Which lenders approve ITIN auto loans in Florida?

Readers frequently ask: “I live in Miami or Orlando. Can I get a car loan with just my ITIN?”

Keys Federal Credit Union (Florida Keys): Keys FCU runs a dedicated ITIN Lending Program that explicitly covers auto loans, personal loans, and credit cards. Unlike larger institutions where ITIN policies can be difficult to pin down, Keys FCU spells it out clearly on its website. Spanish-friendly services and in-person support are available. Membership is geographically limited to those who live, work, worship, or attend school in the Florida Keys, so applicants generally get more direct attention than they would at a national bank.

For borrowers in other Florida cities (Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville), Lendbuzz operates nationwide and is currently the most accessible no-FICO-score online option. Dealer finance networks in the Miami metro also run ITIN programs aimed at the region’s large Spanish-speaking community.

How much down payment do I really need?

This one comes up a lot, especially for first-time buyers who are stretching their savings.

Most ITIN lenders require a down payment of 10%-20% of the vehicle price, or a minimum of $1,000. A larger down payment reduces the lender’s risk and improves your approval odds.

The logic is straightforward: when a lender cannot rely on a long U.S. credit file, they want to see that you have skin in the game. A 20% down payment on a $25,000 used car means walking in with $5,000. That is not a small ask for a recent immigrant or seasonal worker, but it is the clearest variable you control.

What if you cannot afford 20%? Some lenders, particularly online specialty lenders and ITIN-specific credit union programs, will work with 10% down if your income documentation is strong. Bringing a creditworthy co-signer can sometimes reduce the threshold, though it is not always required. Lendbuzz, for instance, explicitly does not require one: it takes only your individual information into consideration for loan terms. The best approach is to get pre-approved by two or three lenders before visiting a dealership, so you know exactly where you stand.

How does Lendbuzz work differently from a credit union?

Lendbuzz is the clearest alternative when no local credit union program is accessible. Its AIRA technology evaluates over 50 billion data points to assess your financial picture rather than relying on a FICO score tied to an SSN. Because many ITIN holders have thin or no traditional credit files, this approach can approve borrowers who would not have a credit score at all under conventional underwriting.

Lendbuzz does not publicly publish its exact APR range. Based on available research and customer reports, rates typically fall between 8% and 29% APR, depending on income, banking history, loan term, and the vehicle being financed.

Vehicle eligibility has specific limits: the vehicle should have a model year from the last nine years, fewer than 120,000 miles, and no salvage title.

Will getting an ITIN auto loan help me build U.S. credit?

Yes, but only if you choose the right lender. Credit bureau reporting is not automatic, and not every ITIN lender does it.

Lenders that report to U.S. credit bureaus will log every on-time payment to your credit file, which is one of the most direct ways for new arrivals to build a U.S. credit profile. Red River Credit Union is one lender that explicitly states it reports ITIN auto loan payments to the bureaus.

Before you sign any auto loan agreement, ask the loan officer one direct question: “Do you report this account to Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion?” If the answer is yes, every on-time payment builds toward a credit score that will help you qualify for better rates on future loans, credit cards, or eventually a mortgage. What should I watch out for when shopping for an ITIN car loan?

A question we hear often: “How do I know if a dealer or lender is trying to take advantage of me?”

There are three specific warning signs to know before you walk into any dealership or sign any loan documents:

1. Rates above 25% APR with no credit history explanation. According to Experian’s 2025 State of the Automotive Finance Market report, even deep subprime borrowers (scores below 500) averaged 22.16% on used vehicles. If a lender quotes you above that without a clear explanation tied to your specific profile, walk away and compare.

2. No credit bureau reporting. A loan that does not report to the bureaus costs you the long-term benefit of credit-building. It is a significant hidden cost that most borrowers do not think about until later.

3. Dealer “spot delivery” without a finalized rate. Some dealerships let you drive the car home before financing is confirmed, then call you days later with a higher rate. Get your final APR in writing before taking possession of the vehicle.

On the debt-to-income side: lenders generally want to see monthly debt payments below 35% of your income, and you will likely struggle to qualify for any loan that pushes your DTI above 50%. Run a quick self-check before applying: add your projected car payment to all other monthly debt obligations, divide by gross monthly income, and make sure the result is well under 0.50.

Can I refinance my current car loan using an ITIN?

Yes, and it can save you real money. If you originally financed through a high-rate dealership arrangement or a buy-here-pay-here lot, refinancing through an ITIN-accepting credit union or specialty lender is worth pursuing after six to twelve months of on-time payments.

If you already have a car loan with a high interest rate, you may be able to refinance through Lendbuzz using your ITIN to lower your monthly payment or reduce your total interest costs. Red River Credit Union also offers ITIN refinancing from other institutions, with a $20,000 minimum. The historical data makes the case plainly: in 2022, clients who refinanced their auto loans with Beneficial State Bank reduced their rates by an average of roughly 9%. On a $20,000 balance, a 9% rate reduction translates to thousands of dollars saved over a 48-60 month term.
