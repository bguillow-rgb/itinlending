## What is an ITIN mortgage, and is it legal to get one in Georgia?

An ITIN mortgage is a home loan where the lender uses your Individual Taxpayer Identification Number instead of a Social Security Number to verify your identity and process the application. These loans are classified as non-QM (non-qualified mortgage) products because they don't meet the conforming loan guidelines set by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, which require SSNs. That classification has nothing to do with your character or creditworthiness. It simply describes how the loan is structured on the secondary market.

No Georgia state law restricts lenders from issuing mortgages to ITIN holders. The non-QM label means the loan is held in a portfolio or sold to private investors rather than guaranteed by a federal agency, and Georgia has a solid network of lenders operating in exactly that space. If you want a deeper look at the mechanics, our guide to [ITIN mortgage loan programs](/articles/itin-mortgage-loan-programs) goes into more detail.

## How much down payment do I need for an ITIN home loan in Georgia?

*This one comes up a lot:* the down payment is usually the largest planning challenge, and the number varies more than most guides admit.

ITIN loans typically require 10%-20% down, verifiable income through tax returns or bank statements, and a minimum credit profile or alternative credit references. In practical terms for Georgia buyers:

- 620+ FICO score: most lenders accept 10%-15% down on a primary residence.
- 600-619 FICO, or no U.S. score: expect 20% or more, with some programs requiring 25%.
- Investment properties or second homes: 20%-25% is standard regardless of credit score.

Some programs require at least 10%-25% down depending on your credit score, plus a two-year work history (W-2 or self-employed) and one year of documented income. Gift funds from a family member can count toward the down payment at many lenders, so ask specifically about gift letter policies when you compare options.

One important note: 20% down on a Georgia home at the current median price (just over $320,000 in the Atlanta metro) means having roughly $64,000 in documented savings. Start building that paper trail now by keeping funds in a verifiable U.S. bank account for at least three months before you apply. If you're still in the savings phase, our overview of [ITIN down payment assistance](/articles/itin-down-payment-assistance) covers programs that may help bridge the gap.

## What credit score and credit history do lenders want?

A 620 FICO score is the standard minimum; 660 gets you better rates at most lenders. But many ITIN borrowers in Georgia have limited U.S. credit history because traditional credit accounts typically require an SSN to open.

Lenders handle that through alternative credit evaluation. Many accept non-traditional credit references: on-time rent payments verified by your landlord, utility payment history, car insurance payment history, and other recurring obligations. Most want at least 3-4 alternative credit references with 12 months of on-time payment history.

Portfolio lenders like First National Bank of America go a step further for borrowers with no U.S. credit at all. Some programs require no U.S. credit score and may use rent history, utility bills, or international credit reports. Documenting your payment history from your home country can strengthen a Georgia application with the right lender.

## What documents will my Georgia lender ask for?

*Readers frequently ask:* the document list for an ITIN mortgage is longer than a conventional loan, but it follows a predictable pattern.

Rather than an SSN card, ITIN borrowers provide their IRS CP565 confirmation letter along with two or more forms of government-issued identification: a valid passport, a consular identification card (Matrícula Consular), a national identity card, or a state-issued ID.

For income, the path depends on how you earn:

- Salaried employees: pay stubs, employer letters, and bank statements.
- Self-employed: 12-24 months of bank statements, a CPA-prepared profit-and-loss statement, or 1099 forms, accepted on most programs.
- Tax returns: two years of U.S. returns filed under your ITIN are non-negotiable at virtually every lender.

Some programs require the ITIN to have been used for tax filing for at least two consecutive years. If your ITIN has expired, fix that before you start shopping lenders. Our step-by-step [ITIN renewal guide](/articles/itin-renewal) walks through the IRS process.

Plan to supply bank statements covering at least 2-3 months, proof of current residence (utility bills or a lease), and employment documentation.

## What are ITIN mortgage rates in Georgia right now?

ITIN mortgage rates in Georgia in late 2026 are running roughly 1-2 percentage points above conventional 30-year fixed rates. With the conventional 30-year benchmark near 6.5%-6.9% in September 2026, most ITIN borrowers in Georgia are seeing quotes in the 7.5%-8.5% range depending on credit profile, down payment, and the specific lender's risk appetite.

Several factors can move your rate lower:

- A down payment at or above 20% signals lower LTV risk to the lender.
- A FICO score of 680 or higher often puts you in a lender's better pricing tier.
- Two full years of consistent ITIN tax filings reduce perceived risk.
- Choosing a portfolio lender or CDFI that specializes in ITIN products gives you more room to negotiate than going through a general broker.

ITIN loans carry no federal backstop, so lenders price in that additional risk. That's the structural reason rates are higher, not anything personal. Our dedicated [ITIN mortgage rates](/articles/itin-mortgage-rates) article tracks current rate ranges monthly if you want to time your application.

## How does the ITIN mortgage process work in Georgia, step by step?

The timeline from first inquiry to closing typically runs 25-35 days with a prepared borrower, slightly faster than the national average for conventional loans according to data tracked by non-QM lenders.

1. Confirm your ITIN is current. Check your IRS CP565 letter. ITINs not used for filing in three or more consecutive years have been expiring since 2017.
2. Pull your alternative credit file. Ask landlords, utilities, and insurance carriers for 12 months of payment records in writing.
3. Gather two years of tax returns. These must be the returns filed under your ITIN with the IRS, not a foreign return.
4. Get a prequalification letter. Apply with two or three ITIN-friendly lenders to compare rate quotes before making an offer.
5. Submit the full application. Once you're under contract on a Georgia property, your lender orders the appraisal and title search.
6. Respond quickly to underwriter requests. Non-QM underwriting involves more manual review; delays usually come from missing documents rather than credit issues.
7. Close and record. Georgia uses attorneys for real estate closings, so budget for attorney fees (typically $500-$1,000) on top of standard closing costs.

## Am I eligible if I am undocumented or do not have a visa?

Lack of legal immigration status does not disqualify a borrower from ITIN loan programs. Lenders are focused on your ability to repay: tax filing history, employment record, credit history, and a meaningful down payment are what matter. That said, some lenders do require a valid visa or immigration document as part of their compliance process, while others don't. Ask each lender explicitly before investing time in a full application.

If you're buying with a co-borrower who has an SSN, that can widen your options considerably. Some programs that would otherwise require 20% down may accept 10% when one borrower on the application has a Social Security Number.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a house in Georgia with only an ITIN and no SSN?**
Yes. ITIN mortgages are legal in Georgia. Non-QM lenders, portfolio banks, and community credit unions all offer programs that use your ITIN in place of an SSN to verify identity and process the loan.

**What credit score do I need for an ITIN mortgage in Georgia?**
Most lenders require a 620 minimum FICO score. Some non-QM programs accept scores as low as 600, and borrowers with no U.S. credit score can qualify using alternative credit such as 12-24 months of on-time rent and utility payments.

**How much down payment is required for an ITIN home loan in Georgia?**
The standard range is 10%-20% for a primary residence. Borrowers with thinner credit profiles or investment properties typically need 20%-25%. Some community lenders accept gift funds toward the down payment.

**What documents do I need to apply for an ITIN mortgage in Georgia?**
You need your valid ITIN, two government-issued photo IDs (passport, consular ID), two years of federal tax returns filed under your ITIN, 12-24 months of bank statements, recent pay stubs or a profit-and-loss statement, and proof of current address.

**Are ITIN mortgage rates higher in Georgia than for conventional loans?**
Yes, typically 1-2 percentage points higher than comparable conventional rates because ITIN loans are classified as non-QM products and carry additional lender risk. A larger down payment and strong income documentation can narrow that gap.

**Can self-employed ITIN holders get a mortgage in Georgia?**
Yes. Most ITIN lenders in Georgia offer bank statement programs for self-employed borrowers, replacing traditional W-2 income verification with 12-24 months of personal or business bank statements and a CPA-prepared profit-and-loss statement.
