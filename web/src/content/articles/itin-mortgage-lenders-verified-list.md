## Why can't I just go to my regular bank for a mortgage?

You can't walk into Chase or Wells Fargo and expect an ITIN mortgage. The reason is structural: the absence of a significant secondary market for ITIN loans is the largest barrier to expanding the market. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac largely don't buy ITIN loans, and the FHA won't insure a mortgage if the borrower can't prove legal U.S. residency.

Because ITIN loans can't be sold to the GSEs, lenders must either hold them in their own portfolio or sell into the private non-QM securitization market. That adds risk, which means higher rates and a much smaller pool of willing lenders. The non-QM segment is growing, though. In May 2026, non-QM reached a 15% share of total mortgage volume, driven by a maturing secondary market that has come to value the credit quality of ITIN borrowers despite their lack of a Social Security number.

The CFPB also notes that not all lenders offering ITIN mortgages advertise them publicly. You may need to contact banks, credit unions, and mortgage brokers directly to ask.

## What if I have no U.S. credit history at all?

This is one of the most common barriers, and it doesn't automatically disqualify you. A traditional U.S. credit score isn't always required. Many lenders will build an alternative credit profile using 12 months of on-time payments for rent, utilities, and insurance.

A lender-acceptable alternative credit history includes on-time rent payments verified by your landlord, utility payment records from electric, gas, water, or phone companies, car insurance payment history, and other recurring obligations. Most lenders want at least 3, 4 alternative references with 12 months of clean payment history.

The key is documentation. Verbal confirmation won't cut it. You need canceled checks, bank transfer records, or letters from verified creditors showing the account number, payment history, and the creditor's contact information. Start collecting these now, even if you're not ready to apply yet.

If you have some U.S. credit history but it's thin, consider reviewing our guide on [building credit with an ITIN](/articles/how-to-build-credit-with-itin) before you apply. Even 6, 12 months of additional history can move you into a lower rate tier.

## How do ITIN lenders verify my income?

*This one comes up a lot:*

Income verification is the central underwriting question on every ITIN mortgage. The good news is that lenders accept more than one method, and self-employed borrowers have real options.

Income can be verified through tax returns, bank statements, or profit-and-loss statements, depending on the lender and employment type. Here's how each works in practice.

Tax returns (full-doc): two years of federal returns filed under your ITIN is the most straightforward path. It demonstrates financial stability and tax compliance, and most programs accept it as the baseline.

Bank statements (alt-doc): self-employed borrowers who write off significant income can qualify using 12, 24 months of business or personal bank statements instead. This is a bank statement loan. Acra Lending, Angel Oak, and NQM Funding all run active bank statement programs that can be paired with an ITIN.

One year of income history: some programs are more flexible than the standard two-year requirement. A minimum two-year work history is generally required (W2 or self-employed), but certain lenders only need one year of documented proof of income.

DTI ratios are capped at lender-specific limits, generally 43%, 50%. Acra Lending allows DTI up to 50%, which opens the door for borrowers with higher monthly obligations.

## How long will it take to close my ITIN mortgage?

ITIN loans typically take 30, 45 days from application to closing, similar to traditional mortgages. Missing documents or complex income situations can push that out. ICE's May 2026 Mortgage Monitor reported a 36.8-day average for purchase loans that closed in March 2026. ITIN loans can hit that benchmark when the file is clean, but manual underwriting means there's less room for error than on an automated conventional file.

The single biggest variable is document completeness at the time of application. Missing documents are the most common reason ITIN mortgage timelines stretch past 45 days.

A realistic timeline looks like this:

- Pre-approval: 1, 3 business days once documents are submitted
- Formal application to underwriting: 7, 14 days (longer for complex income)
- Appraisal: 5, 10 business days depending on local market
- Conditional approval and final conditions: 5, 7 days
- Closing disclosure to closing: minimum 3 business days by federal law
- Total: 30, 50 days for a clean ITIN file

## How do I actually apply? A step-by-step overview

*This one comes up a lot:*

**Step 1: Verify your ITIN is active.** The IRS deactivates ITINs that haven't been used on a federal return for three consecutive years. Check your most recent Notice CP48 or CP565 letter, or confirm with a tax professional. A lender can't process your file with an expired ITIN.

**Step 2: Gather your document package before contacting a lender.** A complete package at the start avoids back-and-forth that stretches timelines. You need:
- Valid ITIN card or IRS letter
- Two government-issued photo IDs (passport, consular ID, or foreign driver's license)
- Two years of federal tax returns filed under your ITIN
- 12, 24 months of bank statements (all accounts, all pages)
- Pay stubs for the last 30 days (W2 employees) or a profit-and-loss statement (self-employed)
- Proof of current address (utility bill, lease)
- Evidence of down payment funds (seasoned at least 60 days in your account)

**Step 3: Build or document your credit profile.** If you have no U.S. credit score, request letters from your landlord, utility companies, and insurance providers showing 12 months of on-time payments. If you want to build credit before applying, a [credit builder loan with your ITIN](/articles/itin-credit-builder-loan) or a [secured credit card](/articles/itin-secured-credit-card) can add tradelines in as little as six months.

**Step 4: Get rate quotes from at least three ITIN lenders.** Use the lender table above to target lenders licensed in your state and suited to your credit and income profile. Collect Loan Estimates (the standard federal form) so you're comparing the same numbers. Rate differences of 0.5%, 1% between lenders on the same borrower profile are common.

**Step 5: Lock your rate once you have a property under contract.** ITIN lenders generally won't lock until the file is submitted and an appraisal is ordered. Budget 30, 45 days from application to closing and set that expectation with your real estate agent and the seller upfront.

If you're buying in Texas, California, or Florida, our state-level guides at [/itin-loans/texas](/itin-loans/texas), [/itin-loans/california](/itin-loans/california), and [/itin-loans/florida](/itin-loans/florida) cover state-specific programs, down payment assistance, and lender contacts in more depth.

## What are the most common reasons ITIN mortgage applications get denied?

ITIN loan approvals often fail for reasons that don't come up in the first conversation with a lender. Down payment thresholds, how income is documented, and whether credit qualifies as acceptable can all sink an application even when the borrower looks fine on paper.

Here are the five most common denial triggers and how to address each.

1. **Expired ITIN.** Lenders can't process a file on a deactivated number. Renew through IRS Form W-7 before applying.
2. **Inconsistent income across documents.** If your bank deposits don't match your tax returns, underwriters will flag it. Make sure your documented income is consistent across all sources.
3. **Down payment funds not seasoned.** Large recent deposits with no paper trail are a red flag. Keep your down payment funds in the same account for at least 60 days before applying.
4. **Too few alternative credit references.** One or two references aren't enough if you have no FICO score. Lenders want at least 3, 4 alternative credit references with 12 months of on-time payment history.
5. **Choosing a lender unfamiliar with ITIN underwriting.** A general mortgage broker who has never processed an ITIN file will slow everything down and may not know which compensating factors to present. Stick with lenders that have documented ITIN program experience.
